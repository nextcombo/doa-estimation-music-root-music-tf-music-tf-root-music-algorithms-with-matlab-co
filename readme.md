# DOA Estimation: MUSIC, ROOT-MUSIC, TF-MUSIC, TF-ROOT-MUSIC Algorithms [with MATLAB Code]

The paper proposes a linear time-frequency multi-signal classification (LTF-MUSIC) algorithm for the separation and direction finding of non-stationary signals. This algorithm is based on the formation of a new array snapsbot by sampling the time-frequency representation (TFR) of the received signal along the signal ridge. Experimental results show that this method has good performance.

In response to this issue, this paper proposes a novel time-frequency Root-MUSIC near-field source localization method. By constructing two spatial time-frequency matrices of the pseudo Wigner-Ville distribution (PWVD), this method introduces a time-frequency Root-MUSIC algorithm to estimate the azimuth and distance of near-field targets. The algorithm has high precision and resolution in estimating the azimuth and distance of non-stationary signals, even under conditions with low signal-to-noise ratio or close source-receiver distance. Finally, the correctness of this method is verified through simulation.
